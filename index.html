01 / EDUCATION SOFTWAREOffline CBT

REXVA V1.3.0

A Windows-based computer-based testing system for schools. Install it on one computer and let students take exams on lab PCs over the local wired or wireless network, with no internet needed to run exams.

  • Multiple choice, true/false and short-answer questions, with question images and CSV or Excel imports
  • Shuffled questions and options, timed schedules, pass marks and server-controlled exams
  • Fullscreen exam mode, one attempt per student, integrity logs and automatic submission
  • Saved answers, recovery after interruptions and automatic backups
  • Instant scoring, printable results, CSV export and optional sync to the online teacher dashboard

Put your REXVA exam videos in this folder using exactly these names:
students-exam-1.mp4  students-exam-2.mp4  students-exam-3.mp4

Tips
- Use MP4 (H.264 video + AAC audio) so they play on every phone and browser.
- Keep each video under about 30-50 MB (1-2 minutes, 720p is enough). Compress with HandBrake or ffmpeg if larger.
- Optional preview image: add a JPG here and set poster:"name.jpg" for that video in index.html (VIDEOS list).
- To add, rename or remove videos, edit the VIDEOS list near the bottom of index.html.
- Big videos? Upload to YouTube (unlisted) and use youtube:"VIDEO_ID" in the VIDEOS list instead of file.
- Only film students who have permission (school/parent consent) to appear on a public website.
